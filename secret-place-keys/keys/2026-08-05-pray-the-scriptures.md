# Pray the Scriptures

Don't know what to pray? Just flip open the Bible and pray according to what you read. Say, "Father, show me" — and flip open the Bible, find something, and turn it into a prayer over yourself, your family, or just intimacy toward God, ministering to Him. It doesn't matter what verse you land on.

**How do you pray according to the Bible?** Take what it says and turn it directly into a request.

Example: Proverbs says something like "he who gives many gifts has many friends." So pray: *"Father, reveal to me how to give many gifts, and help me become a good gift giver. May it appease people and help me find favor in the eyes of others."*

## Highlight prayer verses

Go through your Bible and highlight every verse about prayer with a specific highlighter color. Then, when you don't know what to pray, just flip through and look for that color — and pray according to that scripture.
