# Praying Against Division

Sometimes people are thinking evil or judgmental thoughts toward you, just because the devil likes to stir it up. All it takes is one person not being discerning, and they'll start thinking negative things, speaking negative things, maybe spreading lies — or just carrying negative thoughts. Friendships can end for life because of some unspoken thing that festered in someone's heart, that nobody knew about, that nobody understood was from the devil — and so they just don't want anything to do with you anymore. It happens all the time. The devil is the accuser of the brethren.

So pray against division over your own household — over what people think of you and your household, your friends, your family, your relationships, their thoughts, opinions, and judgments about you. Cover it in prayer with the blood of Jesus.

## The prayer

*"Father, I declare only the Holy Spirit's thoughts about us will reign through in people's hearts and minds when they think about us. Even the pagans, even those who don't believe in the Holy Spirit and Jesus Christ as the risen Son of God, will not be able to think negative things toward us. People will find favor in their eyes toward us. Everywhere we've walked, they will see us with favor. We will be as ones who carry the favor of the Holy Spirit, even from people who don't know Him — they will view us with favor, in the name of Jesus.*

*Every thought of the enemy, every division, every lie, every room someone created for the enemy to speak through them or to them, any part of their mind unyielded to the thoughts of the Holy Spirit — we declare it will not affect our family, in Jesus' name.*

*Father, we understand there is power in people's words. They will not be able to speak negative things over us, and we will never accept negative words spoken over us. We accept all positive words. We declare people will pray for us as we pray for them — increase our desire and hunger to pray for them, as You increase their desire and hunger to pray for us.*

*May we spread peace, love, and positivity. May those who love us spread the same. May those who know us spread the same over us."*
