# Seek First the Kingdom — Daily Prayer

Remind yourself of His victory in His blood, and what its purpose is. Remind yourself that He is the slain Lamb. Ask Him to show you what your purpose is in life. Ask Him to reveal to you the reality of His being. What is the victory?

*"Thank You, Jesus, for Your resurrection power. Help me not to take anything for granted, and not to be downcast, sorry, feeling bad, depressed, anxious, worried — worried about this, worried about that."*

This is the Matthew 6:33 principle: do not worry — seek first the Kingdom of God.

**How do I seek first the Kingdom of God every single day?** It means to build on the foundation of the Kingdom. What is the Kingdom? It is the life, the nature of God within you, wanting to break forth and implement itself across the world. The Kingdom of God is a lifestyle. It's a domain. It's a rulership. It's His Kingdom within us, wanting to break forth out of us and have the final say over what happens on this earth, through that Kingdom.

*"Father, may I seek first — may I build on that foundation first — allowing You to have Your full reign and relationship across my life, and across the entire world through me, across every area through me.*

*Father, teach me. I want You to tell me how I could build on the Kingdom of God, how I could seek first the Kingdom of God. Show me every area of my life where I am worrying, where I am scared, where I am acting as if You're not my Father. Help me, Father — help me act as if I truly am a son or daughter of God.*

*Show me that victory. May I not be so down, knowing I'm going to heaven — reveal to me the reality of the resurrection and of eternity. And Father, reveal to me that I don't have to wait until heaven to live my best life — I can live the new nature out now, because it's within me, in my spirit."*
