# Practical Keys to the Secret Place

Connecting with God in the Secret Place is not about forcing an emotional experience or trying to earn His presence through religious effort. It is about systematically building your born-again human spirit until it is stronger than your natural mind and flesh.

Here are the practical keys and exact "how-to's," drawn directly from the mechanics of the born-again trail.

## 1. Approach from Sonship, Not Slavery

The first key is recognizing your legal right to be in the Secret Place. You are not a beggar standing outside the gates trying to get God's attention — you hold a positional truth in the "bosom of the Father." Jesus prepared a place for you in His glorified humanity, meaning you occupy the exact same room of fellowship with the Father that Jesus does.

**How-to:** Stop approaching God by quoting your "covenant rights" or checking your "check stubs" of recent good behavior. When you enter the prayer closet, start by acknowledging your identity. Boldly walk in, call Him "Abba Father," and commune with Him strictly on the basis that you are a fully-vested joint-heir.

## 2. Pray in Tongues (The Supernatural Bypass)

Praying in tongues is the master key and the foundational "revelation gift." Your natural mind has been trained by the world and does not know how to pray as it ought. Praying in tongues is the divine "idiot switch" where the Holy Spirit completely bypasses your intellect, your exhaustion, and your flesh to pray the perfect will of God directly into your spirit.

**How-to:** Lock yourself in a room and dedicate hours to praying in your supernatural language (Dave Roberson locked himself away for up to six to eight hours a day in his early days). Do not worry if you do not understand what you are saying. You are yielding your earthly authority to the Holy Spirit so He can transfer the mind of Christ and the mysteries of God directly into your inner man.

## 3. Worship Independent of Feelings (The "Lump of Coal" Principle)

True worship in the Secret Place is a positional truth, not an emotional state. The greatest mistake believers make is stopping their worship because they feel spiritually "dry" or hypocritical. Your feelings are connected to your natural surroundings, but your joy and faith are products of your spirit.

**How-to:** Adopt the exact stance of missionary David Hogan: *"My job is to worship it; His job is to receive me."* Even if your flesh feels like a thick "lump of coal" or as dry as a "cracker juice sandwich," you must force yourself to worship Him. God will often retreat to the other side of your flesh to see if you will worship Him purely for His worth. As you persist in worship without feeling anything, that layer of flesh gets thinner and thinner until His manifested presence permanently breaks through.

## 4. Meditate the Word through "Assimilation"

The Holy Spirit uses the Word of God as the medium of exchange to feed your soul. But meditation is not just reading a verse and trying to force your brain to understand it.

**How-to:** Use the law of assimilation. Read entire books of the Bible (like John or Romans) in context, over and over — even 50 times — without aggressively trying to pull a revelation out of them. Let the words suspend in your spirit while you pray in tongues. Suddenly, the Holy Spirit will act as your teacher, popping the revelation into your mind in a millisecond, connecting truths you never saw before.

## 5. Tactical Fasting to Break the Flesh's Megaphone

Your flesh will violently fight your attempts to stay in the Secret Place. Fasting is the physical weapon you use to subdue it. Fasting does not change God's mind, motivate Him, or buy your miracles. **Fasting moves *you*.**

**How-to:** When your flesh screams for attention, use fasting to forcefully execute the positional truth that your body is dead to sin. By starving the physical senses, you break the loud, demanding hold your body exercises over your mind, actively driving your flesh backward so your born-again spirit can soar and connect with the Father unobstructed.
