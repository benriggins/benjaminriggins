# Write Down the First Thing, Even If You Doubt It

I didn't know if the Holy Spirit was speaking to me, but I asked Him to. Something I understood was that He loves me. So when I said "Father, speak to me, Holy Spirit speak to me," I heard: *"Son, I love you."*

My brain immediately thinks that could easily be fabricated — I already know He loves me, so writing down "son, I love you" as if it's the Father speaking feels too easy. That's the easy way out. No doubt He'd have to say that — how do you know it's actually Him and not just you convincing yourself, because you already know it's true?

Nevertheless, I kept writing. I wrote it down: "son, I love you." Then I heard something else, and I wrote it. Then something else, and I wrote it. Then something else, and I wrote it.

But if I never wrote down the first thing — the thing I didn't even think was really Him — I would not be sitting here with three or four sentences worth coming back to.

## Core Principle

Write down the first thing, even if it feels too obvious or too easy to be really Him. It's not about proving the first line — it's the door that opens the rest.
