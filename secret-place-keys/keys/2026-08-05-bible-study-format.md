# Bible Study — Quick Notes Format

A simple format for studying together (or alone).

## 1. Pray together

## 2. Read the passage out loud
Each person reads it.

## 3. Ask three questions
- What stands out to you?
- What do you think God is teaching here?
- How does this apply to our life right now?

## 4. Study deeper
- Are there any repeated patterns?
- Is there a command, a promise, or a result?
- Is there any historical context?
- Does this show anything about our character, or God's character?
- Can you picture this realistically?
- Are there any added elements in the picture that aren't explicitly stated in the Word?

## 5. Write it out
Each person writes a quick paragraph or two on how to live this out — individually or together.
