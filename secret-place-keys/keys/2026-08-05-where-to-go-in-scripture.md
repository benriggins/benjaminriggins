# Where to Go in Scripture

A quick guide for when you know what you need but not where to find it.

- **Something sobering** → Ecclesiastes
- **Wisdom, work, knowledge, instruction** → Proverbs
- **Covenant (be careful with this one)** → Job — Job wasn't under any covenant, so nothing could protect him. Sobering. (Full reflection: [Job and the Need for a Mediator](../secret-place/2026-08-05-job-and-the-need-for-a-mediator.md))
- **Worship, journaling, emotional/soul stuff** → Psalms
- **The tactics of the enemy** → the first few chapters of Genesis, including Cain and Abel
- **Stewardship and investment / wealth transfer** → the story of Joseph
- **Humility** → Pharaoh, who exalted Joseph above himself
- **Leadership** → the story of Moses
- **Jesus, clearly** → the Gospels — keep rereading them, ask Him to reveal how to see Him clearly
- **Inspiration for what a man can do with God** → Elijah and Elisha
