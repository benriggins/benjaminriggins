# The Law of Assimilation

*Expands on point 4 of [Practical Keys to the Secret Place](2026-08-05-practical-keys-to-the-secret-place.md).*

The primary key to reading the Word and meditation is understanding that the Bible is meant to feed your soul so it can agree with your spirit, rather than simply being an intellectual exercise. You can memorize the entire Bible with your natural mind and still not understand its simplest revelations. The Word provides the necessary information, but the Holy Spirit must actively transfer that knowledge into your born-again spirit for it to produce faith and power.

Here's the specific, tactical process — the "law of assimilation."

## 1. Never Take a Verse Out of Context

The greatest mistake in meditation is pulling a single scripture out of its surrounding text. To properly assimilate the Word, you must always back up to where the subject actually begins and continue reading until the thought completely stops. When studying epistles like John, Galatians, Philippians, or Ephesians, read the entire book all the way through repeatedly, because they are written to convey a unified theme and subject.

## 2. Read Repetitively Without Forcing a Revelation (The "50 Times" Rule)

True meditation does not mean staring at a verse and trying to force your brain to extract a deep, hidden meaning. Instead, read the text continually — even up to 50 times — without aggressively trying to pull a revelation out of it. Your only job is to read each verse precisely and acknowledge what it says, even if you don't yet understand its deeper spiritual meaning.

## 3. Suspend the Word and Listen

As you continually read through a book or passage, the Holy Spirit will begin to pick up specific verses that form the outline of the revelation He wants to give you. As you read, you'll eventually feel a specific verse "jump" or "itch" inside your spirit — a signal that the Holy Spirit is trying to illuminate it for you.

When this happens: pause, suspend that verse in the middle of your spirit, and simply listen to Him. Hold the thought still inside you and wait for the Holy Spirit to act as your teacher.

## 4. Couple Meditation with Praying in Tongues

Meditation works hand-in-hand with praying in the Holy Ghost. Praying in tongues releases the mind of Christ inside you, setting your doctrine and giving you the spiritual direction needed to properly divide the Word. When you combine the Holy Spirit ministering insight to your human spirit with you willingly subjecting your mind to the Word, it results in massive transformation.

If you persistently pray in the Spirit while suspending the Word inside your heart, the Holy Spirit can take a passage you've passed over 50 times and suddenly flash the entire, uncompromised revelation into your mind in a millisecond.
