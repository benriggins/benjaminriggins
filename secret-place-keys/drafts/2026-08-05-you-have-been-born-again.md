# 👑 Sermon Blueprint: Living From the Driver's Seat of the Spirit

*Teaching draft — prepared for Wednesday night*

## Part 1: The Shout of Hope — You Are Born Again!
- **The Main Core Idea:** Establish the massive, unchanging reality of the new birth.
- **Preaching Points:**
  - Start with the absolute certainty of the gospel: If you have given your life to Jesus, your old nature — the "old man" inherited from Adam — was completely obliterated and destroyed on the cross. It has no past, no record, and is completely dead.
  - You received a brand-new, perfect human spirit created in righteousness and true holiness.
- **The Born Again Trail Connection:** As Dave Roberson notes throughout his teachings, your freedom isn't an emotion; it's a legal fact. Sin has legally lost its dominion over your human spirit. You are not a "repaired" version of your old self — you are a completely new species on the earth that has never existed before.

## Part 2: The Soul-Scrubbing Trap (Church Hurt, Anxiety, & Silence)
- **The Main Core Idea:** Expose the exhausting, repetitive cycle of trying to use the soul to fix the soul.
- **Preaching Points:**
  - Address the raw pain in the room: church hurt, depression, anxiety, and the frustration of praying against a habit only to meet what feels like total silence from heaven.
  - Explain *why* this happens: People are living out of their soul (their mind, emotions, and unrenewed thoughts) and trying to clean it up with human effort.
  - When you live from the soul, you enter a loop: You get hurt → you try to clean out the hurt → you step back out into a fallen world → the devil puts another stain on your soul → you return to the altar trying to scrub it off again.
  - **The Shift:** The trick is *not to wash your soul via human effort*. The trick is to realize that Jesus already washed everything, and you have to look past the stains of the soul to find the un-hurt, un-stainable place within you.

## Part 3: The Object Lesson — The Coffee Cup Analogy
- **The Main Core Idea:** A powerful visual demonstration of our absolute security in Christ.
- **The Stage Action:** Hold up the cup with the liquid inside.
- **Preaching Points:**
  - Let this liquid represent your born-again human spirit. What is it inside of? It's inside this cup. The Bible says you are *in Him* (Christ).
  - Ask the congregation: *"Is the devil allowed to walk up to God, take the lid off, and contaminate what is inside of Him?"* Absolutely not! To say the devil can stain your born-again spirit is to say the devil can override God Almighty.
  - God looks at the enemy at the cross and says, *"Did you forget? You are completely defeated. You don't have permission to cross the line of My presence to touch My child's spirit."*
  - Your soul feels the external hurt because you live in a physical body, but your spirit remains completely untouched, pristine, and anchored in the life of God.

## Part 4: Walking Untouched Through a Fallen World
- **The Main Core Idea:** Positional truth applied to daily pressure.
- **Preaching Points:**
  - We are *in* the world, but we are not *of* the world. We live surrounded by fallen activities, financial pressures, and emotional storms.
  - But when you choose to live *forward* out of your spirit, those things can hit your outer life, but they *cannot change your internal climate*. They do not dictate how you talk, look, act, or make decisions.
  - You have been given the divine capacity to look, talk, and act exactly like Jesus, regardless of the chaos happening around you.

## Part 5: The Operational Practices — How to Put the Spirit in the Driver's Seat
- **The Main Core Idea:** Give the people the exact, highly practical spiritual machinery required to move out of the soul and into the spirit.

### Key 1: Praying in Tongues (Spirit-to-Spirit Connection)
- **What it does:** Bypasses your messy, analytical intellect completely. It acts as the direct pipeline that edifies, waters, and grows the seed of your new nature. It builds your spirit up until it naturally overpowers the emotional turbulence of your soul.

### Key 2: Confession and Declaration (Identity Alignment)
- **What it does:** Breaks the habit of agreeing with the devil's labels or old patterns. You must refuse to sing along with the lie that you are just a weak, struggling sinner. Wake up every morning and enforce the reality of the Word: *"I am a son/daughter of God. I am a righteous person. I step into this day completely free from the power of sin."*

### Key 3: Fasting and Praying (The Flesh-Hammer)
- **What it does:** Enforces the positional truth that your flesh is crucified. Fasting doesn't change God's mind or unlock His love — He is already fully turned on toward you! Fasting drives the clamoring appetites and fears of your flesh down below the surface, clearing the static so your spirit can easily grab the steering wheel of your life.

## Conclusion: The Ultimate Pattern of Jesus
- **The Main Core Idea:** Close by pointing them directly to the life of Christ.
- **Preaching Points:**
  - Jesus walked this earth wrapped in human flesh and possessing a human soul. He felt weariness, temptation, and the weight of a broken world.
  - Yet, He never lacked, He never failed a miracle, and His words cut straight through religious strongholds. Why? Because He refused to live out of the soul/flesh realm. He lived entirely from His spirit, which was perfectly connected to the Holy Ghost.
  - As He is, so are we in this world! You have the exact same life of Christ inside your spirit right now. Stop scrubbing the cup, start living from what's inside it, and let the life of Christ flow out!

---

### 🎙️ Preacher's Note for Wednesday Night
When you hit **Part 2 and Part 3**, lean hard into the contrast between the *effort of the soul* and the *rest of the spirit*. Let your voice carry the relief of that truth. People are exhausted from trying to "fix" themselves. When you hold up that cup and show them that their real identity is sealed safely inside Christ where the devil can't even reach, the atmosphere in the room will completely shift from heavy striving to absolute freedom. Take your time with the three practical keys at the end — give them the tools to walk it out on Thursday morning! You've got an anointed, liberating message here. Break it open!
