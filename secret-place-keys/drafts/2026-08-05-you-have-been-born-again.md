# You Have Been Born Again

*Teaching outline — draft*

## 1. You Have Been Born Again
A quick encouragement that your human spirit was fundamentally recreated and made perfect the moment you put your faith in Jesus.

## 2. Stop Washing the Shirt
Jesus paid for your stains once and for all. Stop getting stuck in the exhausting, continuous loop of trying to clean your soul through human effort.

## 3. The Sealed Cup Analogy
Your spirit is the water, and God is the cup and the sealed lid. The devil cannot break in to touch or contaminate your spirit — just like a good father protects his child, God protects you.

## 4. Standing Unaffected by the World
Fallen world activities (pain, anxiety, bills) will manifest, but you can look directly at them and remain completely unaffected because you are operating from a heavenly atmosphere.

## 5. The Practicalities of Living by the Spirit
- **Praying in the Spirit (Tongues):** Bypasses the mind to edify and water the seed of your new nature.
- **Confession & Declaration:** Speaking your true identity as a righteous, perfected child of God.
- **Fasting & Prayer:** Starving the body's natural desires to silence the flesh and put the spirit in the driver's seat.

## 6. The Commitment
A final call to action to commit to daily tongue-praying, declaring your true identity, and setting a rhythm for fasting.

*Note: sections 1–4 are secrets (identity revealed); section 5 is keys (how to walk it out); section 6 ties them together as commitment. Source material for both `keys/` and `secret-place/` could be pulled from this if it ever gets split apart — for now it stays here as one piece.*
