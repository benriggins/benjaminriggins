# Secret Place

The secrets — what's *behind* the door once a key opens it. What He actually revealed after applying a key: something the Bible showed you when you read it a certain way, an insight, a shift, an understanding of who He is or who you are.

This is the raw material for future teachings on prayer, the Word, identity, or anything else He reveals — not teaching on how to connect (that's a key), but what was found once connected.

Add entries as dated files, e.g. `2026-08-05-name-of-secret.md`.
