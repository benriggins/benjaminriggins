# Job and the Need for a Mediator

*Connects to [Where to Go in Scripture](../keys/2026-08-05-where-to-go-in-scripture.md) — if you want to know about covenant, go to Job. Job wasn't under any covenant, so nothing could protect him. Sobering.*

Job is literally crying out for a mediator, for a son — *where is Jesus?* He would cry out: "if only I had a mediator, none of this would happen." Because that's all God needs. The old covenant was the foreshadow of the true mediator, which was the blood of Christ. He needed the mediator. We all need the mediator. The mediator is how we make it through this life. Otherwise the devil can do whatever he wants to us.

Adam, in the Garden, gave the devil the right to be ruler of this world — and then we blame God when bad things happen in this world, when bad things happen to us, when bad things happened to Job. No — the devil did that. Not God. God only allowed it to happen because there was no mediator yet. He had no choice. It was law. He was bound by His own word. He gave His word: Adam and Eve, you have dominion. And Adam and Eve gave that dominion to the enemy.

So all the dominion Adam and Eve had — before it was restored to us — guess who had it? Literally the devil. Jesus had to come to restore the dominion back to us, so that the Job situation could never happen again. Ever.

Job was under a different covenant — no covenant at all. He didn't even have the old covenant to protect him. That's why the old covenant had to exist: there had to be some sort of protection, some sort of attribution of righteousness that covered you, some sort of sacrifice that covered you.

## Core Principle

God needing a mediator is a sobering fact. Without one, the devil holds the dominion Adam gave away, and bad things happen that God doesn't cause but is legally bound to allow — until Christ comes as the mediator and restores what was lost.
