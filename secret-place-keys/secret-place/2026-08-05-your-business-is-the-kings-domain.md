# Your Business Is the King's Domain

We use Matthew 6:33 to enforce a guilt trip: "Seek first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you."

Let's be honest — Matthew 6:33 isn't the most popular verse in the Bible because people have a burning desire for righteousness. It's popular because people have a burning desire to never worry about their finances again. We love the second half of the verse.

But look at the life of Jesus. For 18 years, Jesus was building pagan homes in Sepphoris. He was negotiating Roman currency. He was cutting stone. Was Jesus seeking first the Kingdom while He built a Roman house?

Yes. Because He was perfect, and He never violated His own command.

This proves that "seeking first the Kingdom" does not mean "seeking first the prayer closet." It doesn't mean seeking first the church service.

## What Is a Kingdom?

A Kingdom is the King's Domain.

When God gave Adam the earth, it was God's Domain. When Adam sinned, he legally handed the keys of that Domain to the Devil — which is why Satan is called "the god of this world."

But when Jesus came, He didn't just come to take us to Heaven. He came to steal the keys back. He came to re-establish the King's Domain on earth.

And where does that Kingdom live now? Jesus said, "The Kingdom of God is within you."

**You are the Temple. You are the Domain.**

## What That Means Monday Morning

- When you go to work Monday morning, the Kingdom of God walks into the office.
- When you write a sales email with absolute integrity, the Kingdom of God is seeking expression.
- When you manage your employees with the compassion and firmness of a Tekton, the Kingdom of God is advancing.

You do not have to escape your business to find God. Your business is the very ground He has given you to cultivate.

God did not call Adam to escape the Garden; He called him to manage it.
God did not call Jesus to escape the woodshop; He called Him to master it.
And God is not calling you to escape your work. He is calling you to bring the Domain of the King into every square inch of it.
