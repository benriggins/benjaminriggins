# What Do You Think of Me?

> "How precious to me are your thoughts, O God! How vast is the sum of them!" — Psalm 139:17

He has a lot of thoughts toward me. I should ask Him what He thinks of me.

What do You think of me —

- as a dad?
- as a brother?
- as a husband?
- as a son?
- as a businessman?
- as a steward?
- as a human?

— and so much more.

---

**The key:** Ask Him directly, by role, one at a time. Don't assume the answer — let Him actually tell you what He thinks of you in each part of your life.
