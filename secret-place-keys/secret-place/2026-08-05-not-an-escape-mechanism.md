# Not an Escape Mechanism

*Connects directly to [Your Business Is the King's Domain](2026-08-05-your-business-is-the-kings-domain.md).*

It's crazy to me that the presence of God — the Secret Place — has become an escape mechanism for people. An escape from work, at that. An escape from roles and responsibilities.

This makes no sense to me. I believe work, roles, and responsibilities are some of the most enjoyable things in life. I believe those are the tasks God has given us — and that we *get* to fellowship with Him in His presence while we do them!

Perhaps we lack fellowship with the Lord in the midst of our God-given tasks, and that's why we feel we have to escape back to His presence.

Or maybe the work we've been given isn't actually from our Father if it constantly burns us out.

I don't believe Jesus went to the Secret Place as a recharge mechanism. I believe He went because He enjoyed the one-on-one, direct, non-distracted communication with His Father and His Friend.

## Becoming a father

I'm about to be a father, so I understand that this is partnering with my Heavenly Father in His work.

We see fatherhood as a holy responsibility — but what separates entrepreneurship, or work, or labor from that same equation? The first Person of the Godhead is Father. But the second Person, at one point, was a worker. A laborer.

And even the Heavenly Father gave man a garden — to *manage the ground.*

## Core Principle

It was always meant to be in fellowship with Him — your entire life. And it's all supposed to be in the fullness of joy.
