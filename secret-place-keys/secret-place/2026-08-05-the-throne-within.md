# The Throne Within

## What Faith Does — the seed

If the Kingdom of God is within us, and Jesus is seated on the throne of the Kingdom, and we have been given a new heart with a new law written on it… then Jesus speaks the new law. And that law is **ALIVE and ACTIVE**. His words are alive and active and piercing!! Jesus speaks from the throne of the Kingdom *within us*. He speaks that law that's alive.

It's alive and active because it's not just the do's and don'ts of the old covenant. It's the inward, active, specific calling I have to fulfill — the man I have to be — for my exact scenario, requiring specific actions that no one else could follow except me. So it's alive and active.

No templates.
No precursors.
No predictions.
No guesses.

He's living in me, on the throne of the Kingdom, decreeing and declaring what I must do — all the time — that I may **abide in His words.**

I abide in the words of Jesus.
I'm abiding in the living and active sword that pierces.
I'm abiding in the King's commands and decrees — who's sitting on the throne of my heart, which is the throne of the Kingdom.

Meaning: the hearts of all believers are the throne of the Kingdom of God. Because the Kingdom is *within*. Our hearts are within! He's seated on the throne of my heart, in the Kingdom of God, which is within me.

## Wow — he lives in me through my obedience to his law

When I was born again I received a perfect, regenerated, washed spirit. No stains. No sin. New creation. Also meaning — **no excuses**.

So the Spirit of Christ lives in me — not just the Holy Spirit (although He does), but also the same spirit Jesus had: alive to righteousness instead of dead to sin, a perfect Son of God — that lives in me! He's the High Priest not just because He made the sacrifice, but because He guides me into how to live by that same spirit. As I live it out, His life is lived out through me because I'm obedient to the way He lived His life.

## Wait — the hearts of the Bride make up His throne?

I can't get over this: the hearts of the Bride of Christ make up His throne, because the Kingdom is within! And His active voice from that throne room within us — the same voice that is the new law — actively and lively pierces through every element of our entire makeup and creation, to lead and guide us in this new walk of being born again, with the new life and the Spirit of Christ, the hope of glory, manifesting the Kingdom and the King's life!

His throne is within me and on my heart, feeding me the new law I must live by, straight from His voice. That voice of King Jesus on the throne of the Kingdom within — writes the new law.

## How I keep the signal clear: tongues, intimacy, fasting

Three things keep the static out so I can actually hear the King's live voice instead of my own noise:

- **Tongues** — bypasses my analytical mind entirely. Waters the seed with raw revelation instead of me trying to manage it with logic.
- **Intimacy** — not just understanding ideas *about* Him, but becoming beautiful with His nature. Without it, even hearing His voice turns into another checklist.
- **Fasting** — starves the loud, competing noise of the flesh (fear, old labels, natural appetite) so the piercing voice from the throne can resonate without static.

## Cross-reference: Benjamin Riggins, *The Incorruptible Seed*

This realization lines up directly with Riggins' teaching on the incorruptible seed — worth pulling from if this becomes a teaching:

- The Kingdom didn't come as an external message; it came as a literal seed sown into you, carrying "the government, nature, dominion, and life of the King."
- Other seeds — trauma, fear, depression, old labels — got planted in the soul over a lifetime, but none of them are incorruptible. The Kingdom seed is superior and grows up to outgrow and choke them out. This is the "no excuses" — not because of effort, but because the seed inside is unconquerable.
- The growth progression: **understanding → revelation → agreement → obedience → fruit.** Fruit isn't manufactured by striving — it's what happens when a seed is watered and guarded, not panicked over.
- Goal: the Bride, carrying the Kingdom in the earth, "manifest[s] the King" so that when Jesus returns, He returns to a Bride already prepared.
- Result when the inner life breaks out: "the sick are healed, the lepers are cleansed, the dead are raised, the oppressed are delivered, and the works of darkness are destroyed" — not human effort mimicking power, but the King's live decree breaking into physical reality.

**Core shift:** from a checklist religion built by human effort, to a living ecosystem — a seed already sown, a King already on the throne, and a daily rhythm of listening and responding rather than performing.
