# Let Him Flow

What I was looking at felt so forced. Nothing was natural. It felt dried, like leaves.

I'd rather read the Bible and sing in front of it, because online I feel a little alone. When I go in and walk, it's like I'm in a place with headphones — a place of healing. When I allow myself to think and sing along, I stop worrying about whether this is "natural" or "real" worship, and I just sing.

Eventually, when I get there, I have my own self back. Like last week — nighttime, and I decided: "Tonight I'm just going to sing. I'm going to play some worship, open the Bible, and see where this takes me." That's the hard posture — *see where You take me, Holy Spirit.* I don't want to take myself anywhere I want to go. I don't want to put our time together in a box, or in shame.

If I only have a certain amount of time, that's fine. I'm still going to give my dead time to Him. Even in that little bit of time, I have to catch myself not thinking about the clock — not thinking "when is this going to end?" — and instead just let Him flow.

How do you let Him flow? I think if I sit at the table, open the Word, and see where He takes me — that's the place where He starts taking me somewhere. As I yield to that, as I follow the direction He's taking me, I feel more desire to stay, more desire to pray, more desire to continue.

That place is beautiful.

---

**The key:** Stop measuring the time. Stop asking if it's "natural" or "real." Just start — sing, open the Word — and yield to wherever He takes it from there. The desire to stay grows the more you follow, not the more you force.
