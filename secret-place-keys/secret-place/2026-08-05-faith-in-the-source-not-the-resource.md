# Faith in the Source, Not the Resource

*Follows on from [What Faith Does](2026-08-05-what-faith-does.md) — this is a sharper unpacking of where faith is actually placed.*

Faith in what you say shall come to pass. Faith in receiving what you ask your Father for when it comes to your desires. Faith in receiving, and faith in God's willingness and ability to give. Faith in asking and prayer.

You're putting your faith in your own position of receiving, and you're putting your faith in the fact that God will hold fast to His word because of that — to give you what you're in the position to receive.

But it's not faith in what you can say. It's not faith in the power of your prayer. It's not even faith in your own identity. It's faith in the fact that **God will do what He said He will do because you are where you're supposed to be.**

I don't have faith that I will receive from God *because* I am where I'm supposed to be. I have faith that because I am where I am — in the rightful position to receive — and God said that if I am in that rightful position, then my Father is the giver of all good gifts, and that enables me to receive them. I bring that before Him, trusting Him with it, trusting He will do as He says. That is my position of receiving.

So when I tell this mountain, *be removed* — I don't have faith in my word or my prayer. I have faith that God gave me the authority. I have faith in the gift of authority. God doesn't move the mountain — I move the mountain — but I have faith in what God gave me. I have faith that I will receive what God has given me: the authority, in that moment, to remove the mountain.

But it's not even just "in the moment" — it's forever. So how do you separate that? I have an authority, an identity, that can automatically remove mountains. That is my authority and identity. But if I do not have faith in that — if I doubt it — it will not happen.

So obviously, I have to get rid of all my flesh and all my doubts to make it happen, so that all that remains is faith. That's what the testing and refinement of faith looks like — just getting rid of everything but faith. And faith is tied to your spirit, so it's crucifying the flesh to allow the spirit man to live.

But you don't have faith in your spirit man. **You have faith in who God says your spirit man is.**

Do you have faith in what God says about the mountain? What He says about your authority? What He says about your identity? You don't have faith in your identity — you have faith in what God says about your identity.

## Core Principle

**He is the Source that we have faith in. We are the resource that can receive and operate in it. But the resource needs to have faith in the Source.**
