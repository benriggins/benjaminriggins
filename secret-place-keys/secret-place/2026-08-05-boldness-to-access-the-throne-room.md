# Boldness to Access the Throne Room

A reminder that we have boldness and access — by the blood of Jesus — to the throne room. We don't have to wait outside. We don't have to wait for God to come. We don't have to sit around and stare and see what happens.

We have boldness to access. The Father has an invitation. He keeps saying, *come on in, come on in, come on in* — over and over and over. He's waiting on us. We're not waiting on Him. His presence isn't far. We don't have to wait on His presence.
