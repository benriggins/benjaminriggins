# What Faith Does

> "Have faith in God. For verily I say unto you, That whosoever shall say unto this mountain, Be removed… and shall not doubt in his heart, but shall believe… he shall have whatever he says."

> "If you have faith as a grain of mustard seed… nothing will be impossible unto you."

## How It's Connected to the Gospel

> "But as many as received him, to them He gave power to become the children of God, even to them that believe on his name."

## Teaching

Scripture paints faith as the thing that removes mountains, obtains whatever you say, makes nothing impossible to you — and even makes you a child of God. All of it is done through **faith**.

## How to Get It

> "So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God."
